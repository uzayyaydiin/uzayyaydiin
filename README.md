## 🔭 About Me

I am a graduate student in **Astronomy and Space Sciences at Erciyes University**. My research focuses on the **morphological and activity based classification of galaxies**. In particular, I work on identifying elliptical and spiral galaxies, as well as star forming galaxies, Starburst systems, Seyfert AGN, and LINERs using **machine learning, astrostatistics, and large scale photometric data analysis**.

In this context, I develop **scalable and open source Python modules** that can classify galaxies without relying on spectroscopy.

I actively contribute to scientific work within the **LSST Galaxy Science Collaboration** and the **LSST AGN Science Collaboration**.

In addition, I work with **M33 galaxy and MUSE observational data** to deepen my expertise in the **interstellar medium (ISM)** and to better understand its physical properties.

---

## 🌌 Research Interests

- **Galaxy Classification:** Morphology, star formation activity, Starburst galaxies, and AGN
- **Machine Learning in Astronomy:** Classification algorithms and open source Python module development
- **Astrostatistics:** Statistical modeling, large scale data analysis, and model evaluation
- **Interstellar Medium (ISM):** M33, MUSE observations, and the physics of ionized gas
- **Large Astronomical Surveys:** SDSS, LSST, and multi band photometric datasets
- **Scientific Computing:** Python based data analysis, visualization, and reproducible research
